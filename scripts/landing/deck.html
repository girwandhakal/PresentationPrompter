Why trees

Shade is the cheapest cooling we can buy.

Cooler homes

Shaded houses need less air conditioning in July and August.

Less flooding

Roots and soil slow down storm water before it reaches the drains.

More walking

People walk more on streets that feel comfortable at midday.

The plan

Three phases, starting where people walk most.

2026

School routes

400 trees along the walks to Lincoln and Oak Street schools.

2027

Bus stops

300 trees around the 24 busiest stops, where people wait in the sun.

2028–29

Every block

500 trees filling the remaining gaps, chosen with residents.
